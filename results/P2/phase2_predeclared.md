## Fixed choices
- No UE-wrap mask for any condition (consistent with paper 2).
- Metrics, all after the first 2 s:
  - positions: median, p90, and median over epochs with >= 1 blocked O-RU (model-B LoS loss >= 10 dB),
    for the tracker and estimator A (seed level: per-seed value, mean over the 10 seeds; pooled values
    also stored); exact two-sided Wilcoxon over seeds, tracker vs estimator A;
  - Table II windows for the tracker (definitions of scripts/p2_diag_onset.py; estimator A must reproduce
    results/P2/diag_onset_heldout2.json);
  - tracker and estimator A error in the 1 s (10 epochs) after each UE wrap and outside these windows
    (reported as a description, no mask applied anywhere);
  - planner outage minus A5 at 15, 20, 25, 30 dB and the 3GPP reference margin, seed-level exact two-sided
    Wilcoxon over 10 seeds, 95 % cluster-bootstrap CI over seeds (pointwise tests, exploratory).
- All results are reported as they come, whatever their direction.
- The time-shuffle diagnostic is NOT run for this paper.
- New numbers enter the paper only as macros generated by scripts/p2_make_paper.py from the stored results.
